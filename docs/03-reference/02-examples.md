# Examples

Sample prompts to try once connected. The AI assistant will select the appropriate tools automatically.

## Portfolio analysis

> Analyse my holdings. Show the top 5 positions by market value and the sector distribution.

> What is my unrealised P&L today? Which holdings are underperforming?

> Compare my portfolio returns against Nifty 50 over the last 6 months.

## F&O positions

> Group my open positions by underlying and calculate the net delta for each.

> Show the combined P&L and breakevens for my NIFTY straddle.

> What are the margin requirements for all my open F&O positions?

## Market data

> What is the current price of Infosys? Show today's high and low.

> Compare the day's performance of HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank.

> Get daily candles for Reliance Industries for the last 3 months.

## Orders

> Buy 10 shares of INFY at market price.

> Place a limit order to sell 50 shares of SBIN at 820.

> Set up a GTT order: if TCS drops below 3500, buy 5 shares at 3490.

> Show all orders placed today. Are there any rejections?

## Instrument search

> Find all Nifty 50 stocks trading below their 200-day moving average.

> What F&O contracts are available for RELIANCE? Show the option chain.

> Search for ETFs that track gold.
